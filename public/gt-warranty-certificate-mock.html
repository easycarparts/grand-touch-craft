Fair questions

Another shop quoted me 7,000 for full body. Why should I pay 12,900?

That quote is real — for what it is: value film, tucked edges, a one-year workmanship warranty, often no registered manufacturer warranty at all. It's a different product. If price is the deciding factor, take it, genuinely. If the car matters, compare warranties before prices.

What voids the warranty?

Very little, and it's all published: skipping your free yearly inspection converts cover to the 5-year base term, and third-party rework on a panel ends cover for that panel. Impact damage isn't a defect — that's what your free no-fault panel replacement is for.

What film do you use?

Genuine film from our certified roster, chosen for your car and program — with the manufacturer's warranty registered to your car as standard. The film brand warranties the plastic. We warranty the whole job, for life.

Is "lifetime" actually lifetime?

For as long as you, the original owner, keep the car — defined in writing on the certificate. No asterisks you can't read: the full terms are on this site.
