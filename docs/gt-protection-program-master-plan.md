## 1. Why the current model died (and why that's fine)

You built the business on "STEK installed by Grand Touch." That worked when STEK was scarce. Now:

- **The film brand is searchable, so it's comparable, so it's a commodity.** Anyone can Google "STEK full body Dubai" and get a 6,900 quote (Desert Diamond, ex-VAT, published on their site). ZGuard advertises STEK full body **from 7,900**. IPD runs ForceShield full-body promos at **11,399**.
- **ForceShield is STEK's value line** — STEK's own marketing aims it at dealerships and wholesalers. The shops quoting 6–7k aren't magicians: they combine the cheaper film line, scope-shaving (minimal parts removal, 1-year workmanship warranty, panels-only coverage), and in some cases unregistered/gray rolls where the customer never gets a registered manufacturer warranty at all.
- When you sell someone else's brand, **you do the marketing, they keep the pricing power.** Every dirham you spend advertising "STEK" trains the customer to price-shop the word STEK.

**The market structure (from the scan):** full-body sedan pricing clusters at 6.5–7.9k (budget certified floor), 9–13k (thin mid), 14–22k (premium: Topaz 15.3k+VAT, Apex 14k+, PPS ~22k, Approved from 14k), 25k+ (supercar). Full fronts cluster 5.5–8.5k everywhere. There is real, proven demand at 12–16k full body — Topaz Dubai runs a 40,000 sq ft facility on it.

**The proof the pivot works:** Topaz sells film with no manufacturer name anywhere — just "Topaz film" and a **10-year parts-and-labour warranty** — at the top of the market, in Dubai, right now. They took someone else's TPU, branded the install and the warranty, and ended up licensing "Topaz Studios" into the UK's biggest luxury dealer group. The installer brand became the product. That is the lane.

### The four gaps nobody in Dubai fills (your openings)

1. **No shop publishes real lifetime-warranty terms.** "Lifetime" appears as a headline (Approved Automotive, Detail Empire) but nobody shows the document, the claim process, or the exclusions. First shop to publish a signed, downloadable lifetime warranty certificate owns the keyword *credibly*.
2. **No shop documents a parts-off install standard.** Everyone claims "dust-free studio"; nobody films mirrors-off, handles-off, badges-off as a named, tiered standard.
3. **No warranty-verification education.** Nobody teaches customers to demand warranty registration proof or check film serial/QR — the single best weapon against the 6–7k gray offers, unused.
4. **Premium with published prices is an open position.** Topaz is the only premium player with a public menu; Apex/PPS/Papi hide prices. You already proved price-first funnels work (tint). Do it at the premium tier.

## 2. What we sell: the GT Protection Program

### The core mechanism

**The GT Owner's Warranty** — backed by Grand Touch, not a film factory. Film **and** workmanship. Parts **and** labour. On the top tiers: **for as long as you own the car.**

This does four jobs at once:

1. **Kills price comparison.** A competitor can quote "STEK 7k." They cannot quote "GT Lifetime Warranty" — it's not a thing they sell. You've changed the unit of comparison from film brand → warranty issuer.
2. **Kills the film question.** "What film do you use?" → "GT-certified film. We test film from the top TPU manufacturers — the same factories that produce for the big brands — and we only install what passes our spec. Your warranty is with us, in Al Quoz, not a distributor overseas. If it fails, we fix it — film and labour — and here's the signed document."
3. **Layers the risk instead of carrying it.** *(Decided 2026-08-05: no private-label film.)* Genuine branded film on every tier, with the **manufacturer's warranty registered on every single car** — that registration is GT's reinsurance: if the film itself genuinely fails (yellowing, cracking, delamination), the claim goes back to the manufacturer, not GT's pocket. GT self-insures only the workmanship layer and the perks — which a high-skill shop is uniquely cheap at. Bonus weapon: many 6–7k shops sell unregistered rolls, so "every GT car carries a registered manufacturer warranty PLUS the GT Lifetime on top — ask them to show you their registration" is a standing kill shot. (Private-label TPU at $160–440/roll remains a future margin option if ever wanted; parked for now.)
4. **Creates the retention flywheel.** The warranty requires an annual inspection → a guaranteed yearly touchpoint for detailing/tint/ceramic upsell, referral ask, review ask, and content.

### Why "lifetime" is safer than it sounds (the actuarial trick)

- Manufacturer warranties already cover film-defect failure modes (yellowing, cracking, bubbling, delamination) for 10 years. What they never cover is **workmanship** — lifting edges, contamination, stretch marks — which is exactly what customers actually fear, and exactly what a high-skill shop is uniquely cheap at fixing. A house warranty is mostly re-packaging risk the film maker already carries, plus self-insuring redo labour.
- **"As long as you own the car" in Dubai is short.** This is an expat market with fast ownership cycles — average real exposure on a "lifetime, original owner, non-transferable" warranty is realistically 3–6 years. You're selling "forever" and underwriting a typical 4-year window.
- The inspection requirement is the control valve: annual inspection (free, 20 min) within 30 days of the anniversary keeps the warranty valid. Miss it → warranty converts to the base 5-year film-defect cover. You'll see every warranted car every year, catch problems while they're one-panel fixes, and lapsed customers self-remove from the liability pool.
- Price the risk in: notionally reserve ~300 AED per Signature+ job into a warranty fund. At 15 cars/month that's a 54k AED/year reserve against claims that mostly cost you labour hours.

### The three tiers — how you get 2–3 price points without film-brand years

**The axis is the GT warranty term + install spec + aftercare — not the film.** Buyers already think in warranty years (5yr/10yr/12yr films trained them). Keep the mental model, swap whose name is on the certificate. Locally, Desert Diamond already proves 3-tier service-level pricing works (6.9k/10.5k/15.5k on the same car); globally, every top shop tiers by install depth and coverage, never by film grade alone.

**Tier design logic (this is the part that answers your question):**

- **Signature is engineered to be the obvious choice.** The price-to-value gap is deliberately lopsided: for +5k over Essential you get Lifetime vs 5-year, a free no-fault panel replacement, and free wash-and-inspection visits for life. ## 3. Who we sell to, and how we filter

Straight talk on the lead-quality problem: what you're experiencing isn't a nationality problem — it's a funnel problem. A price-led funnel selling a shoppable brand name attracts price shoppers of every passport, and Dubai's wealthiest car buyers include plenty of Indian G63 and Defender owners you'd love to have. Filter on **behavior and budget signals** (which is also all Meta/Google will let you target anyway). Premium positioning does most of the filtering for you.

**The filter stack, in order of impact:**

1. **Price floor in the creative itself.** "Programs from AED 7,900" / hero ads led by "Signature — AED 12,900, Lifetime Warranty." The 5k shopper never clicks. This is the single biggest lever and it's free. (You proved price-first works on /tint-dubai — same pattern, premium altitude.)
2. **Price-first program page.** All three tiers with real prices, Signature featured, warranty document downloadable. No "DM for price" — transparency is the premium signal in this market, because nobody premium does it.
3. **WhatsApp qualifying flow** (buttons, 30 seconds): car model + year → new or used → which program are you interested in (three buttons **with prices visible**) → when do you want it done. Auto-score into the CRM. The program question does the filtering — someone tapping "Essential 7,900" told you their budget without being asked.
4. **Commitment-gated booking (no fee — decided 2026-08-05).** No booking deposit. The commitment mechanism is the calendar itself: a slot is only confirmed with car model, plate, and install week, and capacity is genuinely limited (~15 cars/month) — "I can hold Tuesday–Thursday for you, confirm by tomorrow and it's yours." Real scarcity filters tire-kickers without payment friction.
5. **Scripts, not arguments** (see §4). You never negotiate; you re-frame once, offer the edge-inspection challenge, and walk. Time saved is spent on Signature-signal leads.
6. **CRM lead scoring + routing.** Tag: source × program-interest × car tier × timeline. "Essential-interest + >30-day timeline + no model given" → automated nurture (wa-cards, content links), zero human minutes. "Signature-interest + premium model + this month" → Sean calls within the hour, telegram alert.
7. **Ad-platform layering (weakest lever, still stack it):** luxury-auto interests, affluent-community geo weighting, lookalikes seeded from *closed 12k+ customers* (not all leads — that lookalike is currently learning to find you more price shoppers).
8. **Teach the algorithm with qualified conversions only.** You already fire Meta events from the funnels — change what counts. Fire the optimization conversion only when a lead *qualifies* (program-interest Signature+, or premium model + near-term timeline), not on every raw lead. This is documented practice at high-ticket agencies (Grow My Ads literally blocks unqualified answers from booking and excludes them from conversion counts): every qualified conversion teaches Meta what a real buyer looks like, so the filter compounds inside the auction itself.

**Target avatars for creative (three, not one):**
- **New-delivery premium buyer** (G63, Defender, 911, RR, Land Cruiser/LX, M/AMG, Lucid/BYD premium EVs) — car is 0–30 days old. Hook: "You just took delivery. The next 30 days decide what the paint looks like in 5 years."
- **The burned buyer** — had cheap PPF fail or saw a friend's fail. Hook: exposé content (edges, yellowing, knife lines). The free edge-inspection offer is built for them.
- **The keeper/collector** — holds cars 5+ years or flips premium metal. Hook: the dossier, concours care, panel replacements, "the paint underneath stays day-one perfect."

## 5. GT Owner's Warranty — terms skeleton

*(Draft — final wording needs a UAE lawyer's review before publishing. The legal points below were verified against the actual texts of Federal Law 15/2020 and Cabinet Decision 66/2023.)*

### UAE legal requirements — non-negotiable

- **Art. 11 (Cabinet Decision 66/2023):** it is *prohibited* to advertise a warranty — "orally or in any mean of media" — without giving the consumer the written warranty document. Every Meta ad, reel, or WhatsApp message that says "lifetime warranty" legally requires the document to exist and be delivered. Penalties under the law run up to AED 2M. This is why publishing the full document isn't just marketing — it's compliance, and it's why competitors' vague "lifetime" headlines (Approved, Detail Empire) are legally exposed while yours won't be.
- **Art. 12:** the warranty document must state: supplier details, install date, warranty period, vehicle identification, whether all parts are covered (and repair/replacement price where not free), **the consumer's obligations**, and **clear exclusions**. Your inspection requirement and exclusions are enforceable *because* they're written there — Art. 12 expressly contemplates them.
- **Art. 6:** the tax invoice must state the warranty period, and the warranty document accompanies it.
- **Art. 21 (law) / Art. 34 (regs):** clauses that dodge statutory duties ("no refunds ever," unilateral reinterpretation rights) are void — don't write them; their presence undermines the whole document if challenged.
- **"Lifetime" has no UAE legal definition** — so define it prominently yourself (FTC-style best practice): *"for as long as the original purchaser owns the vehicle identified by VIN on the invoice."* Not the film's life, not the buyer's life.
- Issue the document in **Arabic and English**. Repairs that drag beyond ~7 days can trigger an obligation to provide a substitute — panel rework is a same-day job, so schedule claims accordingly and this never bites.

**Covered (Signature/Concours):** yellowing, cracking, peeling, bubbling, delamination, hazing, edge-lift, and installation defects — film **and** labour, for as long as the registered owner owns the vehicle. Essential: same coverage, 5 years.
**Remedy:** repair or replace film on the affected panel(s) at Grand Touch. One claim per panel per 24 months absent new defect cause.
**Conditions:**
- Inspections at Grand Touch at month 6 and month 12, then annually — each within 30 days of the due date (free; Signature: wash + inspection; Concours: + full ceramic refresh over the film). Missed inspection → coverage converts to the 5-year base term from install date.
- No-fault panel replacements (Signature: 1, Concours: 3): any damage, any cause, one exterior panel per event, film + labour included; panel condition photographed at each inspection; credits void for panels reworked by third parties; non-transferable.
- Original registered owner; vehicle registered in UAE. Quiet goodwill clause (T&C only, never advertised): one transfer permitted within 24 months of installation, with paid transfer inspection (AED 500).
- Wash guidance followed (no automatic brush washes; 7-day cure care per handover sheet).
**Excluded:** impact/accident/stone-through-film damage, vandalism, paint or bodywork failure beneath the film, resprayed panels (disclosed at install), third-party removal/repair/coating over the film, off-road or track incident damage, neglect as defined in the care sheet.
**Claim process (publish it — nobody else does):** WhatsApp us photos → same-week inspection slot → approved claims booked within 14 days → work done free, documented, logged in your dossier.

**Design details borrowed from the shops that run warranties at scale:**
- **Auto-registration in your CRM** at install — the customer does nothing. Tint franchises make customers mail postcards within 30 days or lose coverage; "your warranty is registered automatically before you leave the bay" is both friendlier and a selling point, and you already have the CRM for it.
- **Missed inspection softens, never voids:** coverage converts to the 5-year base term rather than dying (XPEL/Onyx hard-void on missed inspections — softer is safer under Art. 34 and turns the inspection into a welcome checkup instead of a trap).
- **Replacement film runs out the original clock** — it doesn't restart coverage (standard everywhere: XPEL, Tint Club).
- **Aggregate liability capped at the original invoice amount**; no consequential damages (standard: XPEL, Solar Gard).
- **Optional later revenue: a "no-fault" add-on** (US tint shops sell $39–59 one-time accidental-damage cover) — e.g., AED 500–900 at install buys one panel replacement for *any* cause, ever. Pure margin, and it makes the standard warranty's exclusions feel fair.

The certificate is a physical, signed, numbered document handed over at delivery — and a PDF on the site. **Publishing the full terms is itself a marketing weapon in this market** (see gap #1), and per Art. 11 above, a legal prerequisite for advertising it at all.

## 9. Risks & honest caveats

- **The warranty is a promise you must fund and honor.** UAE law binds you to it the moment you advertise it (Cabinet Decision 66/2023 Art. 11 — the written document must exist and be delivered; fines scale to AED 2M). That's a feature — it's exactly why it's credible and why lazy competitors can't copy the headline safely — but it means: lawyer-reviewed terms in Arabic + English before the first ad runs, invoice stating the warranty period, a claims log, a notional reserve (~300/car), and the inspection gate enforced from day one.
- **Registration discipline is the reinsurance.** The film policy (branded film, manufacturer warranty registered on every car — decided 2026-08-05) only protects you if registration actually happens on every single install, including Essential. Make it a CRM checklist item that blocks handover; an unregistered car means GT carries film-defect risk alone.
- **Some buyers will always want the named brand.** Fine — lose them, or install their requested film at Signature pricing with the GT warranty on top. Never lose margin to keep a brand-shopper.
- **Capacity and spec integrity.** The moment a rushed Signature install ships with a tucked edge, the entire premise is cracked. Better a 4-week waitlist (which is itself premium marketing) than a bad panel.
- **Content consistency is the plan.** Every case study that books jobs from content has the owner on camera, repeatedly, for months. If daily filming stalls, the paid funnel still works — but the moat doesn't build.
