## Design rules (locked)

1. **No process jargon on anything customer-facing.** Never: "parts-off," "disassembly," "panel removal," "pre-cut vs bulk," "manual install." Process is explained in content videos and *shown* at the delivery walkaround — the card sells outcomes.
2. **Quality is universal, never a tier.** One line appears under all three tiers — the GT Install Standard — so Essential never reads as the careless option. Tiers differ by warranty, coverage, aftercare, and experience.
3. **Warranty is the price axis.** 5-Year → Lifetime → Lifetime Transferable. It replaces film-brand years as the thing being bought.
4. **Film is described by properties, never by brand** — and the flagship film carrying the lifetime backing gives the warranty delta its "why."
5. **Anchor high:** present Concours first in conversation; Signature carries "Most chosen." Never discount; value is added (Founding 50), never subtracted.

**Translation table (use everywhere):**

| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| Parts-off / disassembly | "Edges wrapped out of sight — the film disappears into the panel gaps" |
| Full disassembly (Concours) | "Concours preparation — a completely seamless, invisible finish" |
| Pre-cut patterns | "Precision-cut for your exact model" |
| Decontamination | "Paint prepared to showroom standard before film touches it" |
| 3-stage QC | "Triple-checked before handover" |
| Cheap film / basic tier | (never — Essential is "our essential program," full stop) |

| Time budget | 2 bay-days | 3 bay-days | 5 bay-days |
| Warranty ops | Register with manufacturer + CRM; month-12 courtesy-check task | + inspection tasks at 6mo, 12mo, then annual; **1 no-fault panel credit tracked in CRM**; ~500 AED to warranty/aftercare fund | Same cadence; **3 panel credits**; ceramic-refresh SKU each visit; ~1,200 AED fund |

Parts-off is an **ops decision inside the spec, not a marketing promise** — the card promises the outcome (invisible edges), the bay delivers it however the car requires.

**Film policy (decided):** no private-label film. Genuine branded film on every tier, **manufacturer warranty registered on every single car** — that registration is GT's reinsurance if the film itself genuinely fails (yellowing/cracking/delamination goes back to the manufacturer, not GT's pocket). Customer-facing, the brand still never leads: the card says "our flagship film," the handover pack shows the registration as proof. Sales weapon: many 6–7k shops sell unregistered rolls — "every GT car gets a registered manufacturer warranty PLUS the GT Lifetime on top. Ask them to show you their registration."

**Film roster (T&C + website "Materials" section — brands as proof, never as the headline):** GT installs films and coatings from its certified roster — currently **STEK, Diamond Pro, Supreme (PPF) and Gyeon (coatings)** — and **reserves the right to select the film that best fits the vehicle and tier specification** from that roster. Every film is genuine and its manufacturer warranty is registered to the car where applicable. This clause does three jobs: brand trust signals on the site (and SEO capture for brand searches), zero commitment to any one supplier, and no quote-shopping on a named film ("the GT Signature spec" is the product, not the roll).

**Aftercare economics (internal):**
- Wash + inspection visit ≈ 80–150 AED cost, 30–60 min — trivial vs the retention/upsell value of a guaranteed touchpoint.
- Ceramic refresh over film (Concours) ≈ 400–600 AED cost per visit (materials + 2–3h). Over a typical 4–6 year ownership ≈ 2.5–3.5k total — priced into Concours' ~9–11k margin. Bonus: all coating work stays in-house forever, which protects the warranty condition (no third-party coatings — the exact clause XPEL uses) and keeps the car in your bay twice a year.
- No-fault panel replacement ≈ 500–900 AED cost per event (panel's film + 2–4h labour). Signature exposure: 1 event, probability-weighted well under 1k. Concours worst case 3 events ≈ 2–3k. Photograph panel condition at every inspection so claims are clean.

## Decided (this session)

- **No private-label film.** Branded film from the GT roster (STEK, Diamond Pro, Supreme; Gyeon coatings), manufacturer warranty registered on every car (GT's reinsurance against genuine film defects). Brand never leads marketing; roster appears in T&C + site Materials section; registration shown as proof at handover. **T&C clause: GT reserves the right to select the film from its roster per tier spec.**
- **No-fault panel replacements:** Signature 1, Concours 3 — any damage, any cause, film + labour. Not transferable.
- **Warranty transferability: never marketed** (Sean: too much risk, buyers don't care). Quiet T&C-only goodwill clause: one transfer permitted **within 24 months of installation**, paid transfer inspection (AED 500). Cards and ads never mention it.
- **Inspection cadence:** 6 + 12 months in year one, then yearly (Signature/Concours; keeps lifetime layer active). Essential: month-12 courtesy check.
- **Aftercare ladder:** Signature = wash + inspection each visit. Concours = wash + inspection + full ceramic refresh over the film, every visit, free (cheap to deliver, high perceived value — deliberately Concours-exclusive).
- **Names:** Essential ✓ and Signature ✓ approved. "Reserve" vetoed (feels cheap) — **Concours** applied provisionally (alternates: Sovereign, Atelier, Bespoke).
