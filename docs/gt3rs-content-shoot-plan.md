# GT3 RS Content Batch — Scripts

**Car:** Porsche 992.2 GT3 RS, in the studio now.
**Installed:** Diamond Pro PCU (15-yr manufacturer warranty, registered) · nano-ceramic window tint · Diamond Pro windshield protection film.
**Format:** Sean to camera, vertical 9:16. Plain text = **say it**. [Brackets] = **do it**. One line = one breath = one cut.

**These are ads, not education.** Every script ends by asking for the message. Nothing in here teaches a viewer how to shop somebody else's quote — if there's a question worth asking, we answer it ourselves and close on it.

**This doc has both lengths.** Part One is the full script per video. Part Two is the 30-second cutdown of each. Shoot the full version line by line; the cutdown tells the editor exactly which lines survive for the reel. Longer cuts work for YouTube/TikTok where they hold.

**How to shoot:** read the script twice, then deliver it line by line — you never need a whole take, just each line clean once. Cut on the line breaks.

## The CTA rule

Every video closes on the same mechanism, worded differently each time so ten videos don't sound like one:

> **One action** — WhatsApp. **One input** — model and year. **One thing back** — the price and the warranty, in writing, same day.

That's the whole funnel and it's already true: prices are published, no callback, no visit needed to get a number. Say it as a fact, not a favour. Video 7 is the one exception — its CTA is footfall (free edge check at the studio), because that offer only works in person.

Never end on "link in bio", "DM us" or "get in touch". Say the action out loud, and let the caption carry the number: **WhatsApp +971 56 719 1045 · grandtouchauto.ae/ppf-dubai**. Bilingual EN/AR.

1. **Get the Diamond Pro warranty registration for this chassis in writing** — shown in video 2, and it makes "15 years" safe to say on camera.
2. **The transfer clause has to be in the published T&Cs before videos 1, 3 and 10 go live.** UAE Cabinet Decision 66/2023 Art. 11: you can't advertise a warranty term you haven't put in the customer's document. The site copy is updated; the signed certificate wording needs to match before these post.
3. No physical GT certificate yet — video 3 is written prop-free. (To-do: a print-ready certificate PDF before the next handover; it's the strongest prop this brand can own.)
4. Change tops between videos so one batch day reads as weeks of content.
5. AC off during takes. Lav mic or phone at chest height. Hex lights behind the car for hero frames.

**Legal line for every script:** no other brand or shop is ever named, and nothing states what "other shops do" as fact. Where the market comes up at all, it's framed as what *we* carry — never as what somebody else fails to.

### Why this holds up if you get challenged

You will get comments on this one. The claims are material science, not brand marketing, and every one is defensible:

- **"Almost all PPF is TPU."** True — TPU is the industry-standard PPF substrate; the tiers below it are TPH and PVC.
- **"TPU comes in two families, one hydrolyses, one oxidises/yellows."** True. TPU soft segments are polyester- or polyether-based. Polyester-urethane is the one vulnerable to hydrolysis (heat + moisture); polyether-urethane is the one vulnerable to oxidative degradation and UV yellowing. Polycarbonate urethane replaces that soft segment and resists both — the carbonate linkage is more stable than the ether linkage in aggressive environments, and polycarbonate diols show the highest hydrolytic stability in hot, humid conditions.
- **The implant line.** Say it exactly as written — *"the chemistry they reach for"*, i.e. the polymer class. PCU is the established biomaterial for long-term implants (Bionate, Carbothane, ChronoFlex) precisely because it survives a hot, wet, oxidising environment for decades. The comparison study is brutal in our favour: polycarbonate-urethane tubing showed minute surface erosion and no major cracking after 10 months in simulated physiological conditions, while polyether-urethane tubing cracked substantially and degraded completely in 45–60 days. **Never say this specific film is medical grade** — say the chemistry family is the one medicine uses.
- **"PCU is harder and less forgiving."** True, and stated openly. Its rigidity is the known trade-off against TPU's conformability on complex curves. Saying it out loud buys you every other claim in the video.
- **The one thing to nail down before you post:** the number on camera must match the registration document in your hand. Diamond Pro's published marketing terms and their spec PDFs have not always agreed. Say what the certificate for *this chassis* says, and hold it up while you say it.

*Deeper cut if you want a bonus line, ideal for comments rather than the video:* when polyester-based film hydrolyses it releases acid, and that acid catalyses more hydrolysis — the failure accelerates itself. Polycarbonate releases CO₂ instead, so there's no runaway.
